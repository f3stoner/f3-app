## 4. Parser Rules and Limitations

- Reused normalization style from prior baseline audits: lowercase, trim, normalize smart apostrophes, normalize `&` to `and`, and strip non-alphanumeric characters for matching.
- Intentionally different logic: this audit parses multi-value Proud Papa cells into tokens, while prior import/audit code treated Proud Papa as a single scalar.
- The parser is conservative. It does not perform fuzzy matching and does not use real-name matching as an automatic acceptance path.
- Parenthetical qualifiers such as `(DR)` and `(2.0)` are preserved while splitting delimiters outside parentheses.
